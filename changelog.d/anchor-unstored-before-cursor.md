- Fix: When the host changes its list before the ingest cursor, moved tool-call rows, results and replaced replies are now stored within a presence window of 10,000 stored rows, 104 million characters of stored text (including tool-call text) and 8 million characters of text in the role being checked, across the session, its verified compression ancestors and its recorded rotation predecessors (all three limits must hold) (#2116, #2455). Tool ids match exactly, including non-ASCII ids. Stored copies are counted before adding rows; a result is never added without its call. The limit is logged at INFO once per session per process. Past the window, these rows stay positional replays as on main, a named loss limit to be fixed in v0.30.x. If a presence read fails, ancestry cannot be verified with state.db present, or a rotation predecessor write failed in this process, the audit keeps its own finds, leaves the other rows as replays and logs a warning.
- Known limit (#2463): where the ancestry only looks complete (no state.db, or a state.db missing the child or parent row) and a predecessor record was never written in an earlier process, missing ancestry can still cause carried rows to be stored again.
