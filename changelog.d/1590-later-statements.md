- New: Opt-in `LCM_RECALL_LATER_STATEMENTS` (default 0) places one later lexical match after each of up to five eligible message hits after final selection, with store-ID links and requested/searched/found/delivered/displaced/ms provenance. Children need an exact ref unseen by the caller in delta mode and a citable ref in reference-strict mode. They bypass the per-session limit; in a full window, the last plain hit moves to the undelivered tail, never a linked source, another child, or the FTS-anchored hit. Children hydrate around their own match with their own allowance, so expanded counts can exceed the expanded-hit limit; `response_char_cap` still applies. Read-only, deadline-bounded, and disabled for internal recall and session delivery.
