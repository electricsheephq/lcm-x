- New: Opt-in `recall_group_by_session` (env `LCM_RECALL_GROUP_BY_SESSION=true`, YAML `lcm.recall_group_by_session`) groups model-called recall hits by session and time, retaining each hit's original `rank` and adding `session_groups` and `sessions_covered`. It is off by default, adds no reads or stored-row writes, and leaves internal recall and tool schemas unchanged. Group fields count toward the response budget; `provenance.session_groups_dropped` counts additional hit removals, including whole session groups in session delivery near its budget.
