- New: opt-in `recall_extra_entries` (`LCM_RECALL_EXTRA_ENTRIES`, default `0`) is a cap (clamped to 25); a call appends at most min(cap, floor(L/2)) compact 300-character extras from ranks L+1.. in snippets hits delivery, where L is the call's delivered limit (`limit` clamped to 25): at cap 25, L=20 → 10 extras and L=25 → 12. Duplicates are skipped without refill. Extras are not counted in `total_results`; the candidate pool and rerank window are still sized from L. Extras carry exactly `kind`, `store_id`, `session_id`, `event_time`, `window`, and `extra=true`; full hits and existing envelope fields stay unchanged. Deadline or read failures report `extra_entries_skipped` (`deadline` or `read_error`); internal recall, answer-ready and session delivery ignore the setting.
